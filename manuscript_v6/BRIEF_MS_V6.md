## The study in one paragraph
57 + 8 methods were scored on 11 public clinical datasets (4 wound, 5 colonoscopy, 1 dermoscopy, 1 thyroid ultrasound), a within-dataset control (AZH train slice) and parametric synthetic wound benchmarks (generators A, B, C; B at texture amplitude 11, 28, 40; three synthetic seeds each). Method groups: U (18 untrained classical), F (14 trained from scratch on FUSeg), K (14, the same 14 trained on Kvasir-SEG), and the modern group. In V6 the modern group is **M19**: 18 ImageNet-pretrained architectures (the original 10 plus 8 new ones), each trained on FUSeg at training seeds 0, 1, 2 and averaged per image over the seeds that exist, plus nnU-Net 2D (one training). Agreement = Spearman correlation of per-method mean scores between two image sets over a method set.

## What the paper now claims (decided; do not reframe)
1. **Composition (pooling) effect, confirmatory, strongest result.** Pooling untrained with modern methods makes wound datasets agree at 0.82 (U+M19) although within U it is 0.37 and within M19 0.21; pooled agreement between wound and other datasets turns strongly negative; stratified agreement (ranking within group before pooling) equals the size-weighted average of within-group agreement exactly (Pearson on within-group percentiles) and removes the effect. Same pattern with F; mirror experiment with K. Report the identity as a short derivation in Methods.
2. **Modality organisation within groups.** Clear for U and K (methods above the floor on non-wound data), present for F. For modern methods it is NOT interpretable: they score near zero Dice on all 7 non-wound datasets (floor table) and their modality difference depends on the training seed (the V5/V6 value 0.46 for the 11 seed-0 models becomes 0.02 with seed 1 and 0.18 with seed 2). Drop the V5 claim "organised by modality within every group".
3. **Among modern methods, rankings are weak and noisy.** Wound-pair agreement 0.21 (hierarchical interval includes 0). Training-seed noise is large: two seeds of the same 16 architectures agree only 0.45 on the same wound dataset. A rule set before the seed experiment (same-dataset cross-seed agreement above 0.7) was NOT met; say so plainly. Still, for Dice the datasets disagree more than seeds do (gap 0.35, interval excludes 0), driven by the original 9 architectures; the 7 new ones show no gap. Neighbouring methods differ by far less than seed noise. Under one common threshold (0.5) wound agreement rises to 0.41 (interval excludes 0 for the change); with per-dataset oracle thresholds it falls to 0.12. The low-agreement statement applies to each model at its own tuned threshold, the protocol used throughout.
4. **Synthetic-benchmark validity follows the method pool.** Generator B amp 40 agrees with the wound data at 0.65 over U+M19 but 0.21 over M19, where wound datasets agree with each other at the same level (0.21; difference near 0). Over U+M19 the benchmark is a worse proxy than another wound dataset (difference -0.17, interval excludes 0). The texture effect appears only in pooled sets. Validity per reference varies widely. Benchmark agreement also varies with the training seed (single-seed groups).
5. **Practical consequences among modern models.** Choosing on one wound dataset and deploying on another loses 0.047 Dice on average versus 0.063 for a random pick; the synthetic pick (nnU-Net) loses 0.030 but changes with the training seed. Mean Dice hides failure modes: on AZH the 18 pretrained architectures miss (Dice < 0.1) 27.9% of non-empty images versus 1.4% for nnU-Net; misses concentrate on heavily padded crops (correlational).
6. **Robustness.** Post-processing variant, NSD tolerance, resolution (FUSeg at 256 px) and empty-mask exclusion do not change claims 1 to 4 (pooled-set robustness from the local rescore: see `rebuild/results/v7/ROBUST_POOLED_V7.md` when it exists).

Contributions to list in the Introduction: (1) measurement of the composition effect in segmentation benchmark agreement with the exact stratification identity; (2) demonstration that a synthetic benchmark's measured validity follows the method pool and the clinical reference; (3) evidence, with 18 architectures at three training seeds, that rankings among modern models on wound data are dominated by small differences and training noise, with practical selection consequences and a failure-mode analysis that mean Dice hides.
