## Summary verdict

- **No wrong number in the manuscript.** Every printed number equals its JSON source after rounding, and every fact key means what its sentence says: right method set, metric, pair category, dataset and interval type. Exceptions are listed below and are about wording or labels, not values.
- `facts_v6.json` is current. Regenerating it from the JSON gives 2,181 facts, identical. The docx, the text extract and `build_v6.js` + facts agree token for token. There are no em dashes and no ASCII minus signs before digits.
- **Independent recomputation** from the per-image files with my own code: 289 of 292 compared values are bit-identical to the facts. The 3 others are a definition detail of the size-weighted average (n_g weights against n_g − 1/n_g), with differences of 1e-4 or less and no change to any printed digit. Split-half reliability with my own random splits gives the same printed range.
- **1 critical issue, in Online Resource 1, not in the manuscript**: the Table S9a caption says all 8 FUSeg duplicates match training images (correct: 7 and 1).
- **4 major issues** (misleading wording or interval labels): abstract "different wound datasets agreed less (0.10)"; Table 5 interval types; "texture raises validity only in pooled sets" (U+K does not show it); "19 method means span 0.07 to 0.20".
- **15 minor issues** (wording, labels, cross-document bases).
