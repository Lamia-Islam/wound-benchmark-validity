## What was analysed

- Sources: local rescore files (`results/v7/rescore_local`, 32 local methods, unrounded) and the Kaggle rescore files (`results/kaggle2/rescore`, rounded to 5 decimals). Exclusions from `results/exclusions_v6.json`; FUSeg 256 uses the FUSeg exclusions.
- Groups: U 18 untrained, F 14 trained from scratch on FUSeg (split by `ALL_METHOD_FAMILY` as `perimage_v6`), M19 19 (18 pretrained architectures averaged per image over the seeds that exist, plus nnU-Net 2D), K11 11 (the paper's K without logreg_all_kv, gbm_all_kv, logreg_colour_kv, which were not rescored).
- Agreement: Spearman correlation of per-method mean scores between two datasets. Wound pairs: mean of the 6 pairs of medetec, co2wounds, fuseg, azh. Wound vs other: mean of the 28 pairs of a wound dataset with one of the 7 other references. Modality difference: mean of the 16 same-modality pairs minus mean of the 39 across-modality pairs.
- Stratified agreement: methods are ranked within their own group first, ranks become within-group percentiles, and the Pearson correlation of the pooled percentiles is taken (as in `STATS_V7.md` section 7).
- Intervals: method bootstrap, methods resampled within each group, 2000 draws, seed 0, stats_v5 design. One set of draws per method set, used for every condition, so the changes between conditions have paired intervals. Within-group and stratified values use the group parts of the same draw.
- Exact p: relabeling test of the modality difference over all 13,860 distinct assignments of the 11 modality labels; (min) marks the smallest attainable value, 2/13,860.
- Conditions (12): post-processing P0, P1, P2 for Dice and NSD at 2 px; NSD at 1, 3, 5 px and at 1% of the image diagonal (P0); FUSeg replaced by FUSeg at 256 px (P0, Dice and NSD 2 px). P0 is the main pipeline. P1 = threshold, then 5 px closing and opening and small-component removal, for trained methods; for untrained methods P1 is P0, because their main pipeline already is this one. P2 = small-component removal only.
- Composition criteria per pooled set and condition. C1: pooled wound-pair agreement minus the higher within-group value, paired interval above 0. C2: pooled wound-vs-other agreement, interval below 0. C3: stratified wound-pair agreement within 0.05 of the size-weighted within-group average. The effect holds when C1, C2 and C3 all hold. The mirror view applies the same criteria to the colonoscopy domain.

## Limitations

- The Kvasir set is K11, not the paper's K14: the three Kvasir pixel classifiers (logreg_all_kv, gbm_all_kv, logreg_colour_kv) were not rescored on Kaggle. U+K11 therefore has 29 methods, not 32, and its values differ from U+K in `STATS_V7.md`.
- Thresholds are not varied here. The untrained methods produce binary masks with no probability map, so a threshold sweep cannot be applied to every method in a pooled set. The threshold analysis for modern methods alone is in `ROBUST_V7.md`.
- P1 changes only the trained methods: for untrained methods P1 is P0 by definition (their main pipeline already uses closing and opening). So in U+M19, U+F and U+K11 the P1 condition moves only one group.
- Conditions are varied one at a time: P1 and P2 are crossed with Dice and NSD at 2 px only, the NSD tolerances use P0 only, and FUSeg at 256 px uses P0 only. Only FUSeg was resampled.
- Intervals resample methods only. They do not include image sampling, so they understate the total uncertainty.
- The exact relabeling p is computed for each condition separately; no correction for the 12 conditions is applied. The smallest attainable value is 2/13,860 = 0.00014, so sets at that floor cannot be ordered by p.
- The composition criteria are summaries chosen for this report: C3 uses a fixed tolerance of 0.05, and C1 and C2 depend on 95% method-bootstrap intervals.
- The Kaggle rescore values (M19, K11) are rounded to 5 decimals and nnU-Net in them is a re-prediction of the run-1 checkpoint, so pooled values with M19 or K11 can differ slightly from `STATS_V7.md` (see Checks); the local values are unrounded.
- M19 is near the Dice floor on the 7 non-wound references (`ROBUST_V7.md`), so its own rankings there carry little information; in pooled sets this floor is part of what separates the groups.
