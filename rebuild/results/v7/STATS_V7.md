# STATS V7: modern methods at three seeds and 19 modern architectures

Generated by `scripts/v7/stats_v7.py` from `scripts/perimage_v7.py`. All numbers in `results/v7/stats_v7.json`. Port of `scripts/v6/stats_v6.py` (same definitions, same random seeds, same bootstrap sizes).

An earlier, independent V7 implementation (older loader API) is kept in `scripts/v7_morning` and `results/v7_morning` and is used only as a cross-check.

## Summary in plain language

- **Modern methods across the wound datasets.** Mean agreement over the 6 wound pairs was 0.126 for V6 M (seed 0 only). With every architecture averaged over its seeds it is 0.274 for the same 11 methods (M11s) and 0.211 for all 19 modern methods (M19, hierarchical interval [-0.102, 0.494]). NSD: 0.308 (M), 0.561 (M11s), 0.473 (M19). Agreement stays low and the intervals are wide (for Dice they include 0); the V6 value 0.126 sits inside them.
- **Mixed pools hardly move.** U+M wound-pair agreement 0.801 (V6) becomes 0.821 with U+M19 (Dice) and 0.866 becomes 0.884 (NSD). ALL57 0.688 becomes ALL 0.772 (Dice).
- **Modality difference.** Mixed pools: U+M 1.089 becomes U+M19 1.101 (exact p 0.00014 (min)). Modern methods alone: M 0.461 becomes M11s 0.329 and M19 0.225 (exact p 0.00577, hierarchical interval [-0.011, 0.404]); the 8 new architectures alone show none (N8s 0.055, p 0.32886). The V6 value for M depends on seed 0: the same 11 methods trained with seed 1 give 0.024 and with seed 2 0.180.
- **Seed noise is large.** Two training seeds of the same 16 architectures, scored on the same wound dataset, agree only 0.446 on average (Dice; method bootstrap [0.177, 0.653]); on the control pair azh~azh_train_slice they agree 0.825. Spread of the 19 modern method means on a wound dataset is of the same order as the seed-to-seed spread of one architecture (section 10d).
- **Seed averaging does not raise cross-dataset agreement.** Wound-pair agreement of the 16 architectures: single seeds 0.177 (mean of the 3 seeds), seed averages 0.179, difference 0.002 [-0.146, 0.175] (Dice). NSD: 0.441 vs 0.546, difference 0.105 [-0.104, 0.284].
- **But the dataset still matters beyond seed noise (Dice).** Same dataset, different seed: 0.446. Different wound dataset, different seed: 0.100. Gap 0.346 [0.110, 0.562] (method bootstrap), hierarchical [0.117, 0.609]. For NSD the gap is 0.143 [-0.014, 0.310] and its interval touches 0. The gap comes from the 9 original architectures (0.493); for the 7 new ones it is 0.055, so among the new architectures cross-dataset disagreement is no larger than seed-to-seed disagreement.
- **Corrected for both noise sources** (16 architectures, seed averages), Dice wound-pair agreement 0.179 becomes 0.396 raw or 0.272 with each pair capped at 1 (additive rule; product rule 0.344 / 0.266). NSD: 0.546 becomes 1.016 raw, 0.822 capped. So for Dice the low agreement is mostly real; for NSD most of the gap to 1 can be explained by noise. These corrections are fragile (section 10c).
- **Are modern methods too close to rank?** Within one dataset, partly yes: on the wound datasets their mean Dice values sit within a few hundredths of each other, the typical gap between neighbours in the ranking is far smaller than the seed-to-seed spread, and a single-seed ranking is only moderately reproducible. A 3-seed average is reproducible enough to rank the clearly better and clearly worse architectures, not the middle of the pack.

## What changed from V6

- New data: seeds 1 and 2 of the 10 ImageNet-pretrained architectures (run 2) and 8 new architectures at seeds 0, 1, 2 (run 2). Three trainings diverged and are absent: manet_r18_in seed 2, manet_r50 seeds 0 and 2. So manet_r18_in@seeds averages 2 seeds and manet_r50@seeds is a single seed (seed 1).
- New method sets: M11s, M19, N8s, U+M11s, U+M19, ALL (U+F+K+M19, 65 methods). All V6 sets are recomputed and match V6 exactly.
- New section 10 (seeds): seed-to-seed ranking reliability, single seed vs seed average, disattenuation for seed and image noise, spread of method means.
- Extended: leave-one-dataset-out for M11s, M19, N8s; leave-one-method-out for M19; stratified analysis for U+M11s, U+M19, ALL; by-image split reliability for ALL, M, M11s, M19; floor table for the new groups.
- The bootstrap tables below show the method-only interval regenerated with the stats_v5 design at B 2000 (seed 0) for every set, so V6 and V7 sets are comparable. V6's tables showed the stored stats_v5 interval, which is B 1000 for modality difference and same dataset; both versions are in the JSON (`method_only_repro_B1000`, `method_only_repro_B2000`, `v5_method_only`).
- Correction to the brief: the brief quotes Medetec split-half reliability 0.962 by photograph. V6 (ALL57, Dice, Spearman-Brown median over 500 photograph splits) is 0.959; by image 0.985. The architectures present at all three seeds are 9 of the original 10 and 7 of the new 8 (16 in total), not 9, 6 and 15: only manet_r50 is missing seeds among the new 8.

## Definitions

- **data**: scripts/perimage_v7.py load(); V6 images, exclusions and units (photograph for Medetec and AZH incl. control, image otherwise)
- **agreement**: Spearman (Pearson on average ranks) of per-method mean scores between two datasets over a method set
- **pairs**: 55 pairs of the 11 REFS; same modality 16 (of which 3 within source: fuseg~azh, kvasir_disjoint~kvasir_sessile, cvc_clinicdb~cvc_colondb; 13 different source), across modality 39; control = azh vs azh_train_slice
- **modality_diff**: mean same-modality pair agreement (16) minus mean across-modality agreement (39)
- **modality_diff_excl_within_source**: mean of the 13 different-source same-modality pairs minus mean of the 39 across-modality pairs; relabeling test drops the 3 within-source pairs by identity (52 pairs)
- **seed_average**: per image, mean of the per-image scores over the seeds that exist (perimage_v7 '<arch>@seeds'); method mean = mean of the seed means
- **method sets**: U (18), F (14), K (14), M (11), U+F (32), U+K (32), U+M (29), ALL57 (57), M11s (11), M19 (19), N8s (8), U+M11s (29), U+M19 (37), ALL (65)
- **random seeds**: split halves 500 unit splits, seed 1; by-image split seed 2; hierarchical bootstrap B 1000, seed 6060 (unit draws first, then method draws pool by pool in the order U, F, K, M, U+F, U+K, U+M, ALL57, M11s, M19, N8s, U+M11s, U+M19, ALL; the V6 pools come first so their draws are unchanged); method-only bootstrap stats_v5 design, seed 0 per pool, B 1000 and 2000; seed analysis: architecture bootstrap B 2000 seed 7070, hierarchical B 1000 with architecture draws seed 7071 and the section-3 unit draws.
- **interval notation**: m[...] = method-only bootstrap (methods resampled within group, stats_v5 design, B 2000, seed 0); h[...] = hierarchical bootstrap (methods within group and units within dataset, B 1000, seed 6060); all 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles.
- Units per dataset: medetec 38/152, co2wounds 150/150, fuseg 112/112, azh 118/150, kvasir_disjoint 120/120, kvasir_sessile 120/120, cvc_clinicdb 150/150, cvc_colondb 150/150, etis_larib 150/150, ph2 150/150, tn3k_test 150/150, azh_train_slice 89/103 (units/images).

### 10c. Disattenuation for seed noise and image-sampling noise

Model: observed method mean = true value + seed error + image-sampling error, independent. Split halves of the same trained models share the seed error (it is a property of the trained model) and differ in image error, so the split-half reliability R_img = (T + S/k) / V. Two seeds on the same images share the image error and differ in seed error, so the Spearman-Brown seed reliability of a k-seed average R_seed = (T + I) / V. With V = T + S/k + I, the reliability of the k-seed average is

    R = R_img + R_seed - 1 = T / V   (additive rule)

    r* = r_AB / sqrt(R_A R_B)

R_img = Spearman-Brown median over the 500 unit splits for the same columns (seed-averaged columns for the seed average; mean over seeds of the single-seed values for single seeds); R_seed = Spearman-Brown of r-bar for 3 seeds, r-bar itself for one seed. The product rule r_AB / sqrt(R_img,A R_seed,A R_img,B R_seed,B) is shown as well; it corrects less. Limits: the additive rule is exact only if the image error is fully shared across seeds; any seed-specific part is counted twice and the rule then over-corrects. Both rules are written for Pearson correlations and are approximate for Spearman. With 16, 9 or 7 architectures the reliabilities themselves are noisy, and when R is small the correction explodes, so single pairs can exceed 1. Values are reported raw and with each pair capped to [-1, 1] before averaging; undefined when R <= 0 for either dataset.

### 10e. Conclusion on the seed question

1. Seed noise is a large part of the picture. One seed of the 16 architectures reproduces another seed's ranking on the same wound dataset at only 0.446 (Dice) and 0.463 (NSD). The V6 value 0.126 for M was one draw from this noisy process: the same 11 methods give 0.291 with seed 1 and 0.232 with seed 2.
2. Averaging 3 seeds does not raise cross-dataset agreement: 0.177 vs 0.179 (Dice), 0.441 vs 0.546 (NSD); both intervals of the difference include 0.
3. For Dice, wound datasets disagree more than seeds do: gap 0.346, method bootstrap [0.110, 0.562], hierarchical [0.117, 0.609]. After correcting for seed and image noise the Dice wound-pair agreement is about 0.272 to 0.396 (additive rule, capped and raw), far from 1. So the low Dice agreement of modern methods across wound datasets is mainly real, not seed noise. This rests on the 9 original architectures; the 7 new ones alone show no gap (0.055).
4. For NSD the gap is smaller (0.143, method bootstrap [-0.014, 0.310]) and the corrected agreement is 0.822 capped: NSD disagreement among modern methods is largely explainable by noise.
5. Pre-specified reading in `results/v7/BRIEF_V7.md` (same-dataset cross-seed agreement above 0.7 with cross-dataset agreement about 0.1 to 0.3): the first condition fails (0.446 for Dice on the wound datasets; only the control pair, 0.825, passes). Under that rule the claim 'modern models disagree because the datasets differ, not because of training noise' is not supported as stated. The defensible statement is narrower: modern methods are close together and single-seed rankings are noisy, yet for Dice the wound datasets still order the original architectures differently by more than retraining does.

## Caveats and limitations

- Modern sets are small (19, 11, 8 methods; 16, 9, 7 in the seed analysis). Spearman over 7 to 9 items moves in steps of 0.02 to 0.04 and its sampling spread is large; all modern-set intervals are wide.
- Seed averages are uneven: manet_r18_in@seeds averages 2 seeds and manet_r50@seeds is one seed, inside M19 and N8s.
- Seed 0 of the original architectures comes from a different run than seeds 1 and 2; run and seed effects are confounded for those 9 (section 10a).
- Modern methods score near 0 on all 7 non-wound datasets (section 9). Their colonoscopy agreement and modality difference rank floor-level scores and should not be read as evidence about colonoscopy.
- The disattenuated values in section 10c rest on a simple variance model, on Spearman instead of Pearson, and on noisy reliabilities; single pairs exceed 1. Use them as a rough range, not as estimates.
- Units-only and hierarchical intervals for agreement sit partly below the estimate because resampling adds noise that attenuates correlation.
- The by-image split reliability for Medetec mixes the four stored variants of one photograph across halves and overstates reliability, and the photograph split is the one to use.
