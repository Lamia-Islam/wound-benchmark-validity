## Definitions

- Per-image Dice from `perimage_v7.load` (V6 exclusions). Empty reference: Dice 1 if the prediction is empty, else 0. Mean Dice over all kept images.
- Candidate sets. M (V6): 10 pretrained architectures at training seed 0 + nnU-Net. M11s: the same 10 architectures, per-image Dice averaged over training seeds, + nnU-Net. M19: M11s + 8 new architectures (seed-averaged). N8s, P10s: the 8 new / 10 original architectures alone. M19_s0, M19_s1, M19_s2: every architecture that has that training seed, as that single model, + nnU-Net (18, 19, 17 candidates).
- Selection loss A to B: best mean Dice on B minus the mean Dice on B of the candidate ranked first on A. Random choice: mean over candidates of (best on B minus candidate on B).
- Synthetic chooser: generator B at amplitude 11, 28 or 40; mean Dice over the 45 images of each synthetic seed, then mean over seeds 2024, 7, 101.
- Within 0.01 / 0.02 of the best: selection loss <= 0.01 / 0.02 Dice (unrounded).
- Miss: non-empty reference and Dice < 0.1. Miss rate = misses / non-empty images. Computed for each individual trained model; an architecture's miss rate is the mean over its training seeds (min and max shown). A seed-averaged per-image Dice is not used for misses because it is not the output of any model.
- Intervals: unit bootstrap, B = 2,000, default_rng([20261010, k]) with k the dataset index (the V6 draws). Every image of a drawn unit counts once per draw of that unit; the same draws for all models of a dataset. Interval of a group or architecture mean: the same mean taken inside each draw. Units are photographs for Medetec and AZH, images otherwise. These intervals reflect image sampling only, with the models fixed.
- V6 reported no bootstrap interval for miss rates. The figure '23.6% to 38.6%' in V6 is the range over the 10 pretrained models, not an interval. The V6-model intervals below use the V6 draws.

## Limitations

- Selection losses are point estimates, as in V6; no interval is given because the loss of a pick depends on a maximum over candidates.
- nnU-Net has a single training; its synthetic and clinical scores carry the noise of one training run, while the seed-averaged candidates average over up to three.
- manet_r18 has 2 seeds and manet_r50 only 1 (diverged runs). manet_r50 is FUSeg's best M19 candidate on one training.
- Bootstrap intervals cover image sampling only; models are fixed. Intervals for group means do not include seed-to-seed variation beyond the seeds averaged.
- Medetec has 38 photographs, so its intervals are wide; on CO2Wounds-V2 the two top M19 candidates (segformer_mitb2 and nnU-Net) differ by less than 0.001 Dice, so its 'best' is essentially a tie.
