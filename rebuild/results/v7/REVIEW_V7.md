# REVIEW V7: numbers requested by reviews R2 and R3

Generated by `scripts/v7/review_v7.py`; every value is in `results/v7/review_v7.json`. Exclusions: `exclusions_v6.json` (AZH test 146 images in 118 photographs; control 78 images in 71 photographs). Intervals are 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles of the bootstrap draws. Minus signs are U+2212.

## Common definitions

- **Agreement**: Spearman correlation (Pearson on average ranks) of per-method mean scores between two image sets, over a method set.
- **Wound pairs**: the 6 pairs of Medetec, CO2Wounds-V2, FUSeg and AZH. **Different-source wound pairs**: the 5 pairs without FUSeg~AZH (one clinic).
- **Seed sets**: P9 = the 9 original architectures with 3 training seeds, N7 = the 7 new architectures with 3 seeds, C16 = P9 + N7.
- **Same-dataset cross-seed agreement**: for each wound dataset, the mean over the 3 seed pairs of the agreement between seed a and seed b on that dataset; then the mean over the 4 wound datasets. **Cross-dataset cross-seed agreement**: the mean over 6 ordered seed pairs (a different from b) and 6 wound pairs of the agreement between seed a on one dataset and seed b on the other (36 values). **Gap** = same-dataset minus cross-dataset.
- **Units**: photograph for Medetec, AZH and the control; image otherwise.
- **Selection loss** (choose on a, deploy on b): best mean Dice on b minus the mean Dice on b of the candidate with the highest mean Dice on a. **Chooser loss**: mean over the 12 ordered pairs of different wound datasets. **Random loss**: for each wound dataset the mean over candidates of (best minus candidate), then the mean over the 4 datasets.
- **Miss**: Dice < 0.1 on a non-empty reference. Miss rate = misses / non-empty images, per individual trained model; architecture value = mean over its available seeds; group value = mean over architectures.
