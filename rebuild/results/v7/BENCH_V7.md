## Definitions

- Agreement: Spearman correlation (average ranks for ties) of per-method mean Dice between two sets, over a method set.
- bench(R): agreement of generator B at the given amplitude with wound reference R, averaged over synthetic seeds 2024, 7, 101.
- other(R): mean agreement of the other three wound datasets with R. Averaged over the 4 references it equals the mean of the 6 wound pairs ('pairs6').
- diff(R) = bench(R) - other(R). Positive means the synthetic benchmark ranks the methods more like R than the other clinical wound datasets do. The 'mean' row averages Medetec, CO2Wounds-V2, FUSeg, AZH.
- Groups. U 18 untrained, F 14 trained from scratch on FUSeg, K 14 trained on Kvasir, M (V6) = 10 ImageNet-pretrained architectures, training seed 0, + nnU-Net. P10s = the same 10 architectures, each scored as the per-image mean over its training seeds 0, 1, 2. N8s = 8 new architectures (unet_r50, deeplabv3p_r50, upernet_convnext_t, unet_swin_t, segformer_mitb2, unet_effb3, fpn_regnety016, manet_r50), seed-averaged the same way. M11s = P10s + nnU-Net. M19 = P10s + N8s + nnU-Net. nnU-Net has one training.
- Diverged trainings (no scores): manet_r18 seed 2, manet_r50 seeds 0 and 2. So manet_r18_in@seeds averages 2 seeds and manet_r50@seeds is a single model (seed 1).
- Single-seed groups: P10_s0 (10 models, identical to M without nnU-Net), P10_s1 (10), P10_s2 (9), N8_s0 (7), N8_s1 (8), N8_s2 (7), and M19_sk = P10_sk + N8_sk + nnU-Net (18, 19, 17 methods).
- U and F have no per-image synthetic scores, so their synthetic per-method means come from pools_v5 exactly as in V6; this is why only modern and K groups get the hierarchical bootstrap.
- Intervals: percentile 95%. Method bootstrap, B = 2,000, default_rng(0), a fresh generator per method set; methods resampled with replacement within each group, mixtures keep their composition. Hierarchical bootstrap, B = 2,000, default_rng(1); methods within the group plus, independently per dataset, clinical units (Medetec photograph, AZH photograph, otherwise image) and synthetic images. Data-only bootstrap, B = 2,000, default_rng(2); units and images only, methods fixed; context, not the primary interval. These are the V6 methods and seeds.

## What changed from V6

- Loader: `perimage_v7` instead of `perimage_v6` (adds seeds 1 and 2 of the ten original architectures, 8 new architectures with seeds 0 to 2, per-image seed averages, and `load_synth` for synthetic per-image scores).
- New method sets M11s, M19, P10s, N8s, U+M11s, U+M19, U+F+K+M, U+F+K+M19, and single-seed groups P10_s0..2, N8_s0..2, M19_s0..2. Hierarchical and data-only bootstraps for M11s, M19, P10s, N8s.
- New outputs: agreement per synthetic seed (`bench40_per_synth_seed`), seed-effect summary, generator orderings and texture counts for the V7 family (U, F, K, M19, U+F, U+K, U+M19), leave-one-out for M19 and M11s.
- Unchanged: all definitions, U, F, K numbers, bootstrap designs, draws and RNG seeds. The V6 block is re-run and stored under `v6` in the json.
- Main shift: the modern group's apparent advantage of the benchmark over another wound dataset (V6 M diff +0.227) disappears (M19 diff -0.004). Seed averaging of the ten V6 architectures alone moves it to +0.134 (M11s); adding the eight new architectures moves it to -0.004. V6 never claimed the M diff was distinguishable from zero, and that remains true.

## Limitations

- Seed averages use 3 seeds except manet_r18 (2) and manet_r50 (1, a single model). The diverged runs are missing, not imputed.
- nnU-Net has one training, so its seed noise is not averaged out and it appears unchanged in every single-seed group.
- The P10 and N8 single-seed groups have 7 to 10 methods, so their method-bootstrap intervals are wide; the differences between training seeds lie inside them.
- U and F synthetic means come from earlier per-method files (pools_v5), so mixtures with U or F get only the method bootstrap.
- The four per-reference diffs share data and are not independent; the pooled sets mix within-group ordering with between-group difficulty offsets (as in V6).
- Data-only intervals are context only: resampling images pulls Spearman toward zero, so they sit asymmetrically around the estimate.
