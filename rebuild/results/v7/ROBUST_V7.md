# ROBUST V7: do analysis choices change the conclusions?

Generated by `scripts/v7/robust_v7.py`. Every number is in `results/v7/robust_v7.json`.

## Bottom line

Verdict: post-processing, NSD tolerance and resolution do not change either conclusion. The threshold protocol does change the size of the first one: a single common threshold for all models raises wound-pair agreement among modern methods, while removing calibration differences (per-dataset oracle threshold) lowers it.

- Wound-pair agreement among modern methods (M19) stays in the range of the main results under every post-processing variant, NSD tolerance, the resampled FUSeg, and the oracle threshold: Dice 0.115 to 0.256, NSD 0.309 to 0.526 (main results: Dice 0.206, NSD 0.479). No change from the main-result setting has a paired 95% interval that excludes 0.
- Exception, one common threshold: M19 wound-pair Dice agreement rises with the threshold, and the rise over the default is above 0 for every common threshold from 0.45 to 0.90. At 0.50 it is 0.404 [0.139, 0.627] (change 0.198 [0.033, 0.347]); the maximum is 0.625 at 0.90. For K11 no common threshold raises it (largest 0.174).
- Why: the mean Spearman between M19 method means on the wound sets and each method's FUSeg-tuned threshold is 0.27 at default thresholds, 0.55 at a common 0.50 and 0.58 at 0.90. This is consistent with the shared ranking at a high common threshold following how confident each model's probabilities are, a model trait that carries across datasets, rather than the datasets agreeing on which model segments wounds best. With each model's best threshold per dataset the agreement falls to 0.115 (K11 0.114).
- Modality structure: the modality difference stays above 0 in all 72 conditions for both M19 and K11 (lowest lower bound 0.007, thr common K11|0.65). Estimates range 0.185 to 0.453 (M19) and 0.245 to 0.610 (K11). The pooled sets with untrained or from-scratch methods are not in the rescore files and are not tested here.
- Post-processing barely reorders methods in Dice (lowest variant-to-variant rank correlation on a non-floor set: M19 0.900), but more in NSD (M19 0.726 on co2wounds; medetec 0.830).
- Resolution: resampling FUSeg to 256 px shifts M19 Dice by -0.017 and NSD by +0.166 on average (a fixed pixel tolerance is looser at lower resolution); the method ranking on FUSeg changes more for NSD (rank rho 0.682) than for Dice (0.918).
- MedSAM: official preprocessing changes MedSAM's reference-box Dice a lot, in both directions (-0.253 to +0.352; interval excludes 0 on 9 of 12 sets). Prompting with nnU-Net boxes beats nnU-Net in Dice only for SAM on ph2; in NSD only for SAM on azh, azh_train_slice; MedSAM with nnU-Net boxes on no set.
- Images without an nnU-Net box (prediction empty, scored 0 on a non-empty reference): 1 to 35 of 560 wound images and 0 to 581 of 690 colonoscopy images (exact only for empty references).
- nnU-Net on held-out FUSeg training images: Dice 0.8328 (n 148) vs 0.7943 on the FUSeg test set (n 112); difference +0.0385, interval [-0.024, +0.105].

## What was analysed

- Source: run-2 rescore files (`results/kaggle2/rescore`), built by `kaggle2/k2_rescore.py`. They hold only the trained Kaggle models, so the local CPU methods (U, F) are not covered.
- M19: 10 original architectures and 8 new ones, each averaged per image over its available seeds, plus nnU-Net (19 methods, as `perimage_v7.GROUPS['M19']`).
- K11: the paper's K group without the three Kvasir pixel classifiers (logreg_all_kv, gbm_all_kv, logreg_colour_kv), which were not rescored. So K11 has 11 methods, not 14.
- Agreement: Spearman correlation of per-method mean scores between two datasets. Wound pairs: mean of the 6 pairs of medetec, co2wounds, fuseg, azh. Colonoscopy pairs: mean of 10. Modality difference: mean of the 16 same-modality pairs minus mean of the 39 across-modality pairs (11 references).
- Intervals: method bootstrap, methods resampled within the group, 2000 draws, seed 0; the same draws for every condition. Exclusions from `results/exclusions_v6.json`.
- Post-processing variants: P0 = Gaussian smoothing of the probability map, threshold, small-component removal (main results). P1 = threshold, then closing and opening with a 5 px ellipse, then small-component removal. P2 = threshold and small-component removal only.
- `grid_dice` (threshold analysis) is Dice of the P0 pipeline at each grid threshold (`postprocess_prob(p, g)` in `k2_rescore.score_image`), so the threshold analysis keeps P0 post-processing. Dice only; there is no NSD on the grid.
- Colonoscopy, PH2 and TN3K are floor datasets for M19 (M19 mean Dice below 0.1): kvasir_disjoint 0.063, kvasir_sessile 0.022, cvc_clinicdb 0.068, cvc_colondb 0.007, etis_larib 0.023, ph2 0.048, tn3k_test 0.015. Rank agreement on them is close to noise for M19, so its colonoscopy-pair numbers carry little information.
- K11 is weak on the wound sets (K11 mean Dice medetec 0.310, co2wounds 0.286, fuseg 0.080, azh 0.183), so its wound-pair agreement is computed on low scores; on colonoscopy it is kvasir_disjoint 0.688, kvasir_sessile 0.384, cvc_clinicdb 0.449, cvc_colondb 0.318, etis_larib 0.238.

## 6. nnU-Net on held-out FUSeg training images

- Held-out set: FUSeg training images 400 to 549 minus duplicates (n 148); nnU-Net was trained on images 0 to 392 minus duplicates, threshold fixed at 0.5, P0 post-processing. These images were the threshold-tuning set of the other trained models.
- Held-out mean Dice 0.8328 (95% image bootstrap [0.793, 0.867]), median 0.913, 5 images at Dice 0.
- FUSeg test (kept 112 images, run-1 scores used in the paper): mean Dice 0.7943 ([0.737, 0.843]), median 0.907, 8 at 0. Rescore P0: 0.7943.
- Held-out minus test: +0.0385 (independent bootstrap [-0.024, +0.105]).

## Limitations

- Only trained Kaggle models are rescored. The pooled sets of the paper that include untrained (U) or FUSeg-from-scratch (F) methods, and K14's three pixel classifiers, are not covered here.
- The threshold grid is 0.05 to 0.90 in steps of 0.05, and grid Dice exists only for the P0 pipeline and only for Dice.
- The oracle threshold is chosen on the very images it is scored on, so it is an optimistic upper bound, not a deployable setting.
- The oracle is limited by the grid: on medetec and co2wounds 92% and 96% of the individual M19 models have their best threshold at the lowest grid value (0.05), so their true optimum may lie lower and is not covered.
- nnU-Net in the rescore files is a re-prediction of the run-1 checkpoint (per-image Dice differs by up to 3.4e-3). The rescore numbers use it; the main paper uses run 1.
- The no-box counts for non-empty references are bounds, because the nnU-Net masks were not brought back from Kaggle.
- Rescore files store values rounded to 5 decimals.
