## Definitions

- Architecture score on a dataset: mean over images (V6 exclusions applied) of the per-image Dice or NSD of one trained model.
- Agreement: Spearman correlation (Pearson on average ranks, ties averaged) over the architectures present in both vectors. n = number of architectures.

So a seed pair has 17 architectures for (0,1) and 16 for (0,2) and (1,2) (old 10: 10, 9, 9; new 8: 7, 7, 7). nnU-Net has one model and never enters a cross-seed comparison.

## c. Variance split: between architectures vs between seeds

Balanced design: the 16 architectures with all 3 seeds. Between SD = SD of their 3-seed means (n 16); also SD of all 18 seed-averaged means (n 18; manet_r50 is a single seed, manet_r18 a 2-seed mean). Within SD = sqrt(MSW), pooled SD across seeds (16 x 3, df 32); pooled-17 adds manet_r18 (df 33). manet_r50 has one seed and is left out of every within-SD. ICC(1) = (MSB - MSW)/(MSB + 2 MSW): share of the variance of single-seed architecture means that is between architectures. ICC(3) = (MSB - MSW)/MSB: reliability of a 3-seed mean. Negative ICC means seed noise exceeds the architecture differences.

### Plain reading

**dice.** On the 4 wound datasets, two training seeds of the same architectures agree at Spearman 0.455 on average (12 values, 16 or 17 architectures, range 0.112 to 0.703). This is below the 0.7 the brief requires, so the first condition fails. Seed-averaged cross-dataset wound agreement is 0.211 for M19 (n 19, 6 pairs) and 0.175 for M18p (n 18), inside 0.1 to 0.3. The pre-specified claim is not supported as stated. In the matched design, where both comparisons use different seeds, same-dataset agreement is 0.455 and cross-dataset agreement is 0.115; difference 0.340 [0.115, 0.559]. The interval excludes 0: the datasets add disagreement beyond seed noise, but seed noise alone already removes a large part of the ranking. Corrected for seed noise, cross-dataset agreement is 0.253 [-0.318, 0.672] of what seed noise allows. Cross-seed agreement on the non-wound datasets is 0.414 (21 values), on the wound datasets 0.455: the low seed stability is not a floor-dataset artefact; it is present on the wound datasets where the models work. By subset (wound, mean of 12): old 10 0.574 (n 9 or 10), new 8 0.259 (n 7). Per-seed cross-dataset wound agreement (architectures only): seed 0 0.057 (n 17), seed 1 0.258 (n 18), seed 2 0.234 (n 16). A rough Spearman-Brown projection of the wound cross-seed value to a 3-seed mean gives 0.715 (M19 averages 3 seeds for 16 of its 18 architectures).

**nsd.** On the 4 wound datasets, two training seeds of the same architectures agree at Spearman 0.467 on average (12 values, 16 or 17 architectures, range -0.003 to 0.824). This is below the 0.7 the brief requires, so the first condition fails. Seed-averaged cross-dataset wound agreement is 0.473 for M19 (n 19, 6 pairs) and 0.524 for M18p (n 18), outside 0.1 to 0.3. The pre-specified claim is not supported as stated. In the matched design, where both comparisons use different seeds, same-dataset agreement is 0.467 and cross-dataset agreement is 0.329; difference 0.138 [-0.007, 0.294]. The interval includes 0: cross-seed agreement is statistically as low as cross-dataset agreement, so for this metric the modern models' wound rankings cannot be told apart from training noise. Corrected for seed noise, cross-dataset agreement is 0.704 [0.313, 1.022] of what seed noise allows. Cross-seed agreement on the non-wound datasets is 0.354 (21 values), on the wound datasets 0.467: the low seed stability is not a floor-dataset artefact; it is present on the wound datasets where the models work. By subset (wound, mean of 12): old 10 0.667 (n 9 or 10), new 8 0.211 (n 7). Per-seed cross-dataset wound agreement (architectures only): seed 0 0.373 (n 17), seed 1 0.496 (n 18), seed 2 0.429 (n 16).
